# REGRAS

## 4. First-touch email

First-contact emails use American English, contain 45–80 words and 3–4 short sentences, and follow this order:

1. **Who I am:** `Hi, I'm Adilson from Devrefract.`
2. **Brand observation:** Name the brand and state exactly one specific, measured fact from the diagnosis `proof` field. The proof must support the sentence. Do not make taste judgments or claim the business is losing customers.
3. **What I can build:** Offer a possible demo with `I can`, never language implying the demo already exists. Say what the demo would show.
4. **Yes/no question:** End with `Want me to send it over?`

Do not include a URL, link, attachment, image, price, promise, or the word `free`. The subject must include the brand name or another concrete detail. The reviewer rejects an email without a cited proof, an observation unsupported by that proof, a claim that the demo already exists, or a missing yes/no question.

### Good example

**Subject:** Glow MedSpa's three Texas locations

Hi, I'm Adilson from Devrefract. I noticed Glow MedSpa lists three Texas locations—Colleyville, McKinney, and Houston—on its website. I can put together a quick demo of a booking flow for those three locations if you'd like to see it. Want me to send it over?

**Proof:** Glow MedSpa's public website lists locations in Colleyville, McKinney, and Houston, Texas.

### Bad example

**Subject:** A game-changing idea

Hi, I'm Adilson from Devrefract. Your website looks outdated and you're losing customers. I built a free demo and here's the link: https://example.com. Would you like to see it?

This fails because it gives an unsupported judgment and business-impact claim, says the demo already exists, includes a free offer and URL, omits proof, and does not use the required yes/no question.
